Welcome

This dashboard helps officers of the Department of Land Resources identify land acquisition projects that are likely to face delay - and understand why - early enough to act on them.

For each project the system produces a delay-risk score together with a plain-language explanation. The reasoning is grouped under four areas:

  • 1. Acquisition process health SIA, landowner consent, compensation, documentation
  • 2. Legal disputes court cases, stay orders, litigation type
  • 3. Rehabilitation readiness R&R plan, resettlement site status
  • 4. Administrative efficiency officer assignment, district historical performance
How to read this dashboard: every risk flag is decision-support information for an authorised officer to review and verify. It is not an automatic or final decision, and it does not replace field verification or due process.
