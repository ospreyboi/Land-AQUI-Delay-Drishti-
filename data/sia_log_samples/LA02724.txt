Social Impact Assessment Report — Highway Project, Punjab_District_2, Punjab

Consultation process: 3 public consultation meetings were held over the assessment period, conducted at the block development office under the Gram Sabha / local body process as required under the LARR Act, 2013. A total of approximately 213 affected residents and stakeholders attended across all sessions, including 76 women. Attendees represented a cross-section of the affected population, including members of the local women's self-help group, landowning farmers, elected Panchayat members, and representatives of the Scheduled Tribe community.

Consultation notes (mixed sentiment):
  - On information requests: a number of attendees stated they would prefer to review the detailed rehabilitation package documentation before forming a final opinion, and requested a follow-up session.
  - On conditional support: a majority of attendees indicated conditional acceptance of the project, contingent on a revised compensation package being finalized before possession is taken.
  - On livelihood support requests: while there was general acceptance of the project's necessity, several attendees requested specific livelihood support measures such as skill training or preference in project-related employment.

Overall consensus recorded: sentiment is mixed but not adversarial. Conditional support appears achievable subject to written clarification on the outstanding livelihood, timeline, and resettlement queries noted above.
