Social Impact Assessment Report — Power Plant Project, Madhya Pradesh_District_2, Madhya Pradesh

Consultation process: 3 public consultation meetings were held over the assessment period, conducted at the Gram Panchayat office under the Gram Sabha / local body process as required under the LARR Act, 2013. A total of approximately 94 affected residents and stakeholders attended across all sessions, including 20 women. Attendees represented a cross-section of the affected population, including members of the local women's self-help group, representatives of the Scheduled Tribe community, and elected Panchayat members.

Consultation notes (mixed sentiment):
  - On resettlement-specific concerns: concerns raised were largely limited to the readiness of the resettlement site rather than opposition to the project itself, suggesting the core acquisition is broadly accepted in principle.
  - On timeline clarity: residents requested clearer, written timelines for compensation disbursement and physical possession, noting that verbal assurances given during the meeting were appreciated but insufficient for planning purposes.
  - On conditional support: a majority of attendees indicated conditional acceptance of the project, contingent on a revised compensation package being finalized before possession is taken.

Overall consensus recorded: cautious acceptance was observed across most attendees. No major objections were raised in principle, though several procedural clarifications remain outstanding before formal sign-off can be recommended.
