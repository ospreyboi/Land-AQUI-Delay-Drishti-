Social Impact Assessment Report — Highway Project, Bihar_District_4, Bihar

Consultation process: 4 public consultation meetings were held over the assessment period, conducted at a temporary shamiana erected near the proposed site under the Gram Sabha / local body process as required under the LARR Act, 2013. A total of approximately 154 affected residents and stakeholders attended across all sessions, including 39 women. Attendees represented a cross-section of the affected population, including agricultural laborers, members of the local women's self-help group, and landowning farmers.

Consultation notes (majority supportive):
  - On connectivity benefits: residents noted that the project would meaningfully improve regional connectivity and market access for local agricultural produce.
  - On gram sabha resolution: the Gram Sabha passed a formal resolution supporting the acquisition, citing long-term developmental benefits to the panchayat as a whole.
  - On employment expectations: there was broad support for the project given the anticipated generation of local employment opportunities during both construction and operational phases.
  - On compensation satisfaction: several attendees described the compensation package as fair and, in some cases, above their initial expectations, referencing similar recent acquisitions in the region.

Overall consensus recorded: strong community backing was evident throughout the session, with several attendees actively requesting an expedited timeline for project commencement.
