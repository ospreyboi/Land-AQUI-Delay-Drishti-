Social Impact Assessment Report — Industrial Corridor Project, Madhya Pradesh_District_2, Madhya Pradesh

Consultation process: 3 public consultation meetings were held over the assessment period, conducted at the local primary school premises under the Gram Sabha / local body process as required under the LARR Act, 2013. A total of approximately 189 affected residents and stakeholders attended across all sessions, including 66 women. Attendees represented a cross-section of the affected population, including elected Panchayat members, members of the local women's self-help group, and representatives of the Scheduled Tribe community.

Consultation notes (mixed sentiment):
  - On resettlement-specific concerns: concerns raised were largely limited to the readiness of the resettlement site rather than opposition to the project itself, suggesting the core acquisition is broadly accepted in principle.
  - On conditional support: a majority of attendees indicated conditional acceptance of the project, contingent on a revised compensation package being finalized before possession is taken.
  - On livelihood support requests: while there was general acceptance of the project's necessity, several attendees requested specific livelihood support measures such as skill training or preference in project-related employment.

Overall consensus recorded: sentiment is mixed but not adversarial. Conditional support appears achievable subject to written clarification on the outstanding livelihood, timeline, and resettlement queries noted above.
