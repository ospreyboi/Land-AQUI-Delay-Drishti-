Social Impact Assessment Report — Power Plant Project, Karnataka_District_3, Karnataka

Consultation process: 3 public consultation meetings were held over the assessment period, conducted at the block development office under the Gram Sabha / local body process as required under the LARR Act, 2013. A total of approximately 82 affected residents and stakeholders attended across all sessions, including 29 women. Attendees represented a cross-section of the affected population, including local shopkeepers and traders, members of the local women's self-help group, elected Panchayat members, and tenant cultivators.

Consultation notes (majority supportive):
  - On employment expectations: there was broad support for the project given the anticipated generation of local employment opportunities during both construction and operational phases.
  - On process transparency: community representatives specifically commended the transparency of the consultation process, noting that queries raised in earlier sessions had been addressed satisfactorily.
  - On connectivity benefits: residents noted that the project would meaningfully improve regional connectivity and market access for local agricultural produce.
  - On gram sabha resolution: the Gram Sabha passed a formal resolution supporting the acquisition, citing long-term developmental benefits to the panchayat as a whole.

Overall consensus recorded: strong community backing was evident throughout the session, with several attendees actively requesting an expedited timeline for project commencement.
